01 - The Challenge

One spreadsheet turned into a dozen, and none of them agreed.

Over its run, Noisily grew into a 7,000-capacity festival in the woods. That growth turned budget management, from ticket sales through to scenario planning, into a full-time job for festival director Will.

Without a dedicated platform, every department built its own spreadsheet, structured however each manager preferred. None of them fed into the master profit and loss statement automatically, so keeping the numbers accurate meant a labour-intensive manual check across every sheet.

The gap showed up as overspending. Departments went over budget simply because nobody could see the full picture until it was too late to do anything about it.

The old way, every year
No shared structure across department spreadsheets
Nothing fed into the master profit and loss automatically
Manual cross-checking just to catch errors
Overspending discovered after the fact, not during

"Festival budget management is rarely simple. After four years of trying various tools, none of them met our needs or lasted beyond a year."

One platform, not a dozen spreadsheets.

After four years of searching, Will had started to think simple budget management for a festival this size was a pipe dream, until Noisily found Eventwise. Streamlined budget control across departments, live spending visibility, and the ability to test scenarios made it an easy decision.
